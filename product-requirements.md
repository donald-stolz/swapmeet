# Product Requirements

## How do we add value to the online community market?

We add value by being a trusted platform for individuals to thrift within their local communities. Keep transactions local, within a trusted community. Utilize AI tools to help with difficult tasks like pricing and negotiating

## What MVP features do we want?

1. Integrated payments 
2. Buy/offer/swap
3. AI Powered pricing and negotiating
4. Recomendation engine/Product finds

## Who are our users/customers?

Three Groups (Shoppers, Sellers, Businesses), but MVP  focuses on the just shopper/seller experience. Our users enjoy second hand shopping, and likely prioritize local/community businesses and/or environmental/sustainabiliity projects

## Why do we want to build this?

Build a local connected resale economy. Where reusing goods is the easy fun default choice. Showcases communities and culture, highlights what makes them special/unique

### Will we love the product when we are done?

We will love because we’ll get to see awesome communities. We’ll have a meaningful impact on the environment and communities.
