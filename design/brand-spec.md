# Swapmeet — brand and product reference

Local resale marketplace: neighborhood swap-meet warmth, AI-assisted convenience.

## Color palette
- Terracotta `#C15B3D` — primary brand color
- Market paper `#F3E9D8` — base surface / page background
- Chalk white `#FAF6EF` — card / listing backdrop
- Charcoal ink `#2B2621` — body text
- Market mustard `#E8A33D` — signage, tags, secondary accent
- Faded sign teal `#3E7C74` — trust, verified, success states
- Plum accent `#6B3F5C` — AI assist, negotiation
- Sticker coral `#F4623A` — energetic accent, calls to attention
- Citrus green `#A8C23B` — discovery, recommendations

## Typography
- Display / signage: **Permanent Marker** (Google Fonts) — wordmark, banners, category headers
- UI / body: **Nunito**, weights 400/700 (Google Fonts) — everything else, including AI copy
- Tags / stamps: **Special Elite** (Google Fonts) — trust badges, price tags, receipts

## MVP features (confirmed)
1. Integrated payments
2. Buy / offer / swap
3. AI-powered pricing and negotiating
4. Recommendation engine / product finds

## Launch audiences
- **Shopper** — treasure hunters, buy-local-first, environmentally conscious
- **Seller** — clearing a closet, vintage/collectible dealers, want a fair fast price
- Deprioritized to phase two: thrift stores, other businesses, live-stream selling

## Mission
Build a local, connected resale economy where reuse is the easy, fun default.
Showcase communities and culture — highlight what makes each one unique.

## Tone and voice
Warm, eclectic, optimistic, slightly playful, human-first, contemporary.
Avoid: sterile Silicon Valley aesthetics, generic eco-green/leaf/recycling icons,
crypto/web3 visual clichés, dystopian or robotic AI imagery, luxury-resale polish.

## Iconography (Tabler icon names)
`arrows-exchange` swap · `credit-card` payments · `sparkles` AI assist ·
`shield-check` trust · `tag` price tag · `compass` recommendations ·
`map-pin` local · `message-2` community voice

## Listing card pattern
Photo swatch → title + price/seller line → trust badge → AI price/negotiate chip →
"Recommended for you" strip → action row: **Buy now** / **Make offer** / **Swap**
